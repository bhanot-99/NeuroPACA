Synapse Firing & Potentiation

“A path is not built, then used; it is built by being used.”

When two neurons fire in close temporal succession, neurotransmitter vesicles cross the synaptic gap, physically multiplying receptor channels. Repeated signaling cements the route — unused pathways wither.

Biology: Long-Term Potentiation (LTP) · “Fire together, wire together”

Hebbian Graph Strengthening

Built

“Association weights grow with co-activity, decaying with uptime into forgotten quiet.”

When apps are used together, their graph link strengthens saturating at 1. During daemon uptime without co-occurrence, unused edges decay with a half-life of 24h — math directly copying biological plasticity.

Formula: w ← w + ηc(1 - w) · w(t+Δt) = w(t)·2^(-Δt/T_half)

The hippocampus rapidly indexes lived experiences as they happen, binding sights, sounds, and sequences into coherent episodic traces before neocortical sleep consolidation.

Episodic Event Stream

Built

“Contradicting an old fact never deletes it; it simply closes its validity interval, preserving total truth.”

Every activity shift is written to an append-only SQLite log with validity intervals ([t_valid, t_invalid)). Stale facts are invalidated rather than erased — keeping historical truth completely intact.
